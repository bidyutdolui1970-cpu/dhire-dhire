# Historical notes sample for the Dhire Dhire project.

## Ancient India

The Maurya Empire rose after the decline of Alexander's influence in the northwestern subcontinent. Chandragupta Maurya, with the guidance of Chanakya, built a powerful centralized state. The empire later expanded under Ashoka, who converted to Buddhism after the brutal Kalinga War.

## Medieval India

The Delhi Sultanate was established after successive Muslim invasions and the rise of Turkic rule. It was marked by military campaigns, administrative changes, and the development of Indo-Islamic culture. The period also saw the growth of trade and the construction of major monuments.

## Modern India

The Indian National Congress was founded in 1885 and became the primary platform for the nationalist movement. Leaders such as Gandhi, Nehru, and Patel shaped the struggle against colonial rule. Independence in 1947 led to the creation of India and Pakistan, followed by major political and social restructuring.
